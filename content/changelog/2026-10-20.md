---
title: Hero Quest fixes
description: Smaller income bursts, whole Void Shards, an Arena reload fix, an Auto Roll reserve and Collections browsing.
date: 2026-10-20
---

- **Hero Quest**: Treasure Hunter's Gambit and King's Ransom now grant a few seconds of income per cast instead of a few minutes.
- **Hero Quest**: prestiges pay whole Void Shards, so a balance no longer falls a fraction short of a Shop price.
- **Hero Quest**: reloading the page during an Arena fight no longer shows a server error.
- **Hero Quest Traits**: Auto Roll keeps 100 Trait Gems, so you can always load a saved board after a bad run.
- **Hero Quest Collections**: step to the previous or next entry from the detail screen with the arrows, the arrow keys or a swipe.
- **Hero Quest**: the Witch Doctor's Raise Dead now strikes every enemy as well as healing the party.
- **Hero Quest Classes**: every class now makes your Hero stronger than the one it was taken from.
