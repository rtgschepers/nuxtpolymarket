- **Hero Quest**: Treasure Hunter's Gambit and King's Ransom now grant a few seconds of income per cast instead of a few minutes.
- **Hero Quest**: prestiges pay whole Void Shards, so a balance no longer falls a fraction short of a Shop price.
- **Hero Quest**: reloading the page during an Arena fight no longer shows a server error.
- **Hero Quest Traits**: Auto Roll keeps 100 Trait Gems, so you can always load a saved board after a bad run.
